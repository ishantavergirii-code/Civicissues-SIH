Welcome to the Civic Sense Guide

This guide helps citizens understand the importance of civic sense, cleanliness, and responsibility towards society. By following simple habits, everyone can contribute to a healthier, safer, and more responsible community. Together, we can make our cities cleaner, greener, and more livable for all.

1. Keep Your Surroundings Clean

Dispose of waste properly in dustbins and segregate recyclable and non-recyclable materials. Avoid littering in streets, parks, rivers, and public spaces. Encourage your neighbors and friends to do the same. Participating in community clean-up drives is a great way to make a positive impact and inspire others.

2. Respect Traffic Rules

Follow traffic signals, pedestrian crossings, and speed limits. Wear helmets and seat belts, and avoid using mobile phones while driving. Respecting traffic rules reduces accidents and ensures the safety of everyone on the road. Educate children and family members about safe road behavior.

3. Preserve Public Property

Do not damage benches, streetlights, playgrounds, public buildings, or signage. Vandalism increases maintenance costs and degrades community spaces. Report damaged public property to authorities and participate in restoration or beautification initiatives whenever possible.

4. Save Water and Energy

  • Turn off taps when not in use and fix leaks promptly.
  • Switch off lights and appliances when not needed.
  • Support and adopt renewable energy sources whenever possible.
  • Teach family and friends about conserving resources.

Conserving water and energy reduces environmental strain and ensures sustainability for future generations.

5. Be Polite and Respectful

Respect others’ opinions, be courteous, and avoid conflicts in public spaces. Simple gestures like greeting neighbors, helping elderly or differently-abled people, and maintaining calm during disagreements foster a harmonious community. Being respectful strengthens social bonds and creates a safer environment for everyone.
